abstract: >-
  MotorMind connects a general-purpose vision-language model's mid-level action
  proposals to deterministic robot control and execution feedback, with
  asynchronous monitoring and background memory updates. Without task-specific
  policy training, it achieves 66.7% success on base LIBERO-PRO suites, 53.8%
  under perturbations, and 95% average success on a real xArm6 robot across
  direct manipulation and human-perturbation settings.
